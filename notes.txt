Brand Guidelines

Why we exist? Mission, vision or purpose. Some brands have all, others only one. I like just one answer.
Who we are? Think of it like values, principles, beliefs or philosophy.
What we do? This is usually a descriptive, but differentiating statement. It could be a USP.
How do we express ourselves? Personality. Bold? Sophisticated? Down to earth? Disruptive?
